Our story

And then, somehow, it became us.

Some stories are too personal to reduce to a few lines on a website. So this space is intentionally simple: a place for the memories, words and moments that brought Prudence and Promise here.

“The best part of this story is not how it started. It is that we get to keep writing it together.”

So, on a Sunday morning, 19 May 2024, I was going to church after being absent for a week because I had fallen sick. It was a very chilly day. I had these black snow boots on, a turtleneck top and a long deconstructed skirt.

I got into church just before they started singing the hymns. I looked to my left, towards the opposite direction, like the other end of the world, and there was someone looking directly at me. And I was confused. I was wondering, why is this person looking at me? I think I've always seen this kind of thing in movies, when a fugitive is hiding from someone. Maybe they have changed towns, changed their address, and then, from afar, they find somebody looking directly at them. And there's this panic in their heart like, “Who has discovered me in my hiding place?” So basically, that was how I was feeling. Someone was looking at me, and I was wondering if we'd met before.

The service progressed, and somehow, he moved and sat directly behind me during the service. Anyway, that was his first time there and at the end of the service, he approached me. He asked for my name and then, somehow, asked about my PhD. That was a funny part because nobody was aware about my PhD at that time. Like, how did he even know to ask me that? I would find out later.
We exchanged numbers, and then we got talking as friends and that was how we met.

From, Prudence POV
